# Methodological bibliography

## Diachronic semantic change

- Hamilton, W. L., Leskovec, J., & Jurafsky, D. (2016). *Diachronic Word Embeddings Reveal Statistical Laws of Semantic Change*. ACL 2016.
- Hamilton, W. L., Leskovec, J., & Jurafsky, D. (2016). *Cultural Shift or Linguistic Drift? Comparing Two Computational Measures of Semantic Change*. EMNLP 2016.
- Kutuzov, A., Øvrelid, L., Szymanski, T., & Velldal, E. (2018). *Diachronic word embeddings and semantic shifts: a survey*. COLING 2018.

## PPMI and factorization

- Levy, O., & Goldberg, Y. (2014). *Neural Word Embedding as Implicit Matrix Factorization*. NeurIPS 27.

## Bias and controls

- Dubossarsky, H., Weinshall, D., & Grossman, E. (2017). *Outta Control: Laws of Semantic Change and Inherent Biases in Word Representation Models*. EMNLP 2017.

## Domain-dependent sentiment

- Hamilton, W. L., Clark, K., Leskovec, J., & Jurafsky, D. (2016). *Inducing Domain-Specific Sentiment Lexicons from Unlabeled Corpora*. EMNLP 2016.

These references motivate design choices; they do not make any particular corpus comparison valid automatically. Corpus, period, language, translation, hyperparameters, and concept operationalization must be justified separately.
