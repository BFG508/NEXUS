The crew argues that duty cannot be assigned only by origin or biology. A synthetic being may accept responsibility, show kindness, and protect life. Compassion becomes a relation across distance and difference, while obligation is evaluated by foreseeable harm. Justice depends on transparent systems and the freedom to challenge them. Existence includes memory copied between machines, embodied life, and uncertain continuity. Some reject the language of soul; others use spirit or inner life to describe identity and hope. Fear of the unfamiliar can produce cruelty, but care and fairness can reduce conflict. Peace requires trust between different forms of being.
